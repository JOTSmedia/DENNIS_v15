Kidney surfaces in the introduction originate from BodyParts3D 3.0, converted to STL by Kevin Mattheus Moerman (FMA7204 and FMA7205), under CC BY-SA 2.1 Japan. Separately credited noncommercial kidney and inner-ear assets from Z-Anatomy are excluded.

Adaptations include anatomical selection, curve-to-mesh conversion, limited polygon reduction, material-alpha animation, medical materials, cyan spine treatment, geometry labels and leader lines, camera animation, and removal of skin, reproductive anatomy and obscuring fascia. This is an educational visualization, not a diagnostic model.
